# LXRY — Luxury Yachts L.L.C

Website for Luxury Yachts L.L.C (brand **LXRY**), a Dubai yacht rental and water sports company licensed by the Dubai Maritime City Authority.

It is a static multipage site with no build step on Vercel. The HTML pages are generated by `tools/build.py`, which holds all the content, so edit that file and rebuild rather than editing the HTML by hand:

```sh
python3 tools/build.py        # regenerates all 21 pages
python3 -m http.server 8000   # preview at http://localhost:8000
```

## Pages

Home, Yachts, one page per boat (Jet Boat 38 ft, Yacht 44/45/50/55/80/82 ft, Tarrad 40 ft), Water Sports, Fishing & Events, Gallery, About, FAQ, Contact and 404.

Legal drafts (each starts with "Draft — to be reviewed by LXRY before publishing"): Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Cookie Policy and Booking & Cancellation Policy.

## Deploy

Vercel project `lxry-yachts` is linked to this repo. It has no framework and no build command, and every push to `main` deploys.

## Motion

Buttons are the only animated elements. On press they scale to 0.96 for about 120ms and spring back, with a soft ripple from the click point clipped inside the rounded corners. On desktop hover they lift 2px with a stronger shadow, and social icons also scale up slightly. This covers WhatsApp/Call, the floating WhatsApp button, social icons, the menu button and the photo viewer controls. Nothing else on the site moves. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, only colour changes remain.

## Privacy and third parties

- Fonts are self-hosted in `fonts/` (SIL Open Font License). No requests go to Google Fonts.
- Before consent, no request is made to any third party. The banner offers equal-size Accept and Reject buttons, and the choice is stored in `localStorage` as `lxry-consent`.
- The Google Maps embed (Contact page) and the YouTube video (Gallery page, `youtube-nocookie.com`) load only after Accept, or when the visitor clicks "Load map" or "Play video".
- There are no analytics, tracking pixels, embedded social feeds or forms.
- Every photo's source on lxry.ae is listed in `IMAGE-SOURCES.md`.

## Accessibility

- Checked with axe-core (WCAG 2.1 AA) on every page at 375 and 1440px.
- Text over photos has a measured contrast of at least 4.5:1.
- Keyboard: the menu and photo viewer keep focus inside while open and close with Escape. The FAQ uses native `<details>`, and the focus ring is 2px.

## Design rules

- Palette: near-black `#0D1114`, ivory `#F7F5F0`, platinum `#C9CED6` and deep sea teal `#0E4A52`.
- Never use gold, yellow, amber, mustard, champagne or beige-gold anywhere in the design. Real photo colours are fine.
- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond for headings and Inter for body text.
